The user requested warmer, earthier light surfaces around Zotero RGB 244/236/216
(=#F4ECD8=), and stronger Iceland colors. Other dark palettes are unchanged.
Secondary surfaces retain their previous RGB offsets from the revised background.
Weak light foregrounds were minimally deepened in HSL lightness to retain at
least 4.8:1 on all three base surfaces and on their specified highlight pairs.
These corrections preserve hue families; the exact resulting values live in
=landscape-palettes.el=. Iceland gets explicitly more saturated accents.

assumptions; its recorded measurements remain historical evidence.

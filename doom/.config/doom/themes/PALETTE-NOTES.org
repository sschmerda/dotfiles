#+title: Custom theme palette notes

Shades of Purple dark preserves the installed VS Code source's background,
white foreground, orange keywords, yellow functions/cursor, green strings,
cyan types/parameters and pink constants. Transparent selection/search colors
are flattened onto the editor background, since Emacs theme faces are opaque.
Its comments/docstrings need a small lightness increase to meet 4.5:1 across
all three base surfaces. Gruber retains the reference's charcoal/yellow,
blue functions, green strings, quartz types/constants and brown comments.
A diagnostic red correction preserves readable status text on the active line.
Light variants are original readable adaptations, not official light ports.

The proposed light purple comment/doc color was deepened from =#775095= to
=#734B91= so inactive labels remain readable on the darker modeline surface.

* Rosé Pine and Catppuccin (2026-11-03)
The seven variants retain official base and secondary backgrounds. Syntax hues
follow Rosé Pine's reference syntax roles and Catppuccin's style guide. For
readability in this shared face engine, text colors are adjusted in HSV while
preserving hue: at least 4.5:1 against base, alternate and current-line surfaces;
core light syntax targets 6.5:1, and body text targets 7:1. Catppuccin current-line
backgrounds blend surface0 over base at 45%; diff/search surfaces use 12%/18%
accent blends. These are local Doom adaptations, not exact upstream ports.
